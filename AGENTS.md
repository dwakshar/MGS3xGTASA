# AGENTS.md

**Current phase:** Phase 0 - awaiting approval

## Project summary

- A mod for GTA San Andreas PC 1.0.0 US that adds MGS3-inspired gameplay: stealth, enemy awareness and search, camouflage index, CQC, tactical weapons, scouting, radio, and optional survival.
- GTA SA's map, world, peds, vehicles, and graphics stay as they are.
- MGS3 is a mechanical reference only, not a 100% replica.
- Target: Windows PC, GTA SA 1.0.0 US only.

## Files and folders

- Never modify, move, or delete anything in the original GTA SA folder. All work goes in `mod\` or `docs\`.
- Keep the `backup\` folder untouched.
- Do not publish or upload anything anywhere.

## Assets and copyright

- Do not use any MGS3 files: models, textures, animations, voices, music, codec scripts, or level layouts.
- Use only original content, or assets whose license allows reuse. Record the source and license of any third-party file in `docs\DECISIONS.md`.

## Process

- Follow `docs\ROADMAP.md`. Do not write gameplay code until I approve the current phase gate.
- Log every scope or architecture decision in `docs\DECISIONS.md` with the date and reason.
- Mark untested code as UNTESTED.
- Keep changes small and reviewable, one feature at a time.

## Computer and games

- Do not start, close, or restart any game.
- Do not control my mouse or keyboard.
- Ask me before installing or downloading anything, and before any step that costs money.

## Honesty about technical claims

- Label every technical claim as VERIFIED (with a source link), LIKELY (reasoning only), or UNKNOWN.
- Do not invent CLEO, ASI loader, or GTA SA functions, commands, memory addresses, or file formats. If you are not sure, say so.
- If a research result contradicts the plan, stop and tell me before continuing.

## Asking me

- Ask one question at a time, and only when a decision is needed.
- Tell me what you did, what changed, and what still needs my decision, in short plain sentences.
